Drive Mad is a physics-based car game that tests how well you can control your car on increasingly difficult levels. From simple jumps to zero gravity and everything in between, driving steady matters more than speed.

Drive Mad is the most popular car game on Poki, with a Poki Score of 10/10 according to our latest car games research.

How to play Drive Mad

In Drive Mad, balance matters more than speed. Going fast in this car game feels tempting, but staying in control keeps the car upright through jumps and landings.

To Play Drive Mad, you need to master braking and acceleration. The car tilts forward when you accelerate and backward when you brake. Use this to control your angle mid-air and on slopes.

The first 10-15 levels teach balance and timing. After that, the difficulty ramps up so you better learn from your mistakes fast.

“Drive Mad works because it is simple, funny and a bit chaotic. You instantly understand what to do, but the physics make every level feel unpredictable. Failing is part of the fun, and quick restarts make it easy to try again. It can genuinely drive you mad, in a way that makes you want to press restart immediately.” Robin Gylling, Fancade

Controls

There are only 2 controls to master: acceleration and braking/reversing. This simplicity means mistakes show up fast, so timing matters.

  • Desktop: Press the right key to move forward and the left key to move backwards.
  • Mobile: Tap and hold the arrows to move forward and backwards.

Short taps let you save bad landings and turn near-crashes into perfect runs. Hold the button too long and momentum takes over, turning a clean run into a crash in seconds.

Drive Mad features

Each feature changes how the level plays, catching you out when you get comfortable.

Levels and worlds

Drive Mad has 8 worlds and 365 levels. To finish them all, you need to land the car safely because later levels don’t give you time to fix mistakes.

  • Classic world: This is the main Drive Mad experience, with 200 levels that slowly increase in difficulty.
  • Winter world: This winter-themed world adds a snowy aesthetic to the game with 25 different levels.
  • Monster truck world: This world includes 25 levels where you drive a monster truck with much larger wheels. The added weight makes small mistakes harder to correct, especially in the later stages.

Level mechanics

These 5 common level mechanics punish repeating the same move and force you to play each level differently.

  • Magnet levels: Drive too close and magnets pull the car, demanding instant corrections to avoid crashing.
  • Low or zero gravity levels: Braking shifts the car’s balance instead of slowing it down, making timing more important than speed.
  • Moving machinery levels: Rotating wheels, conveyors, and swinging obstacles knock the car off balance if you enter at the wrong moment.
  • Hazard timing levels: Lasers, shockwaves, and falling objects punish rushed runs and reward patience.
  • Goal-based puzzle levels: Pushing objects and creating pathways from obstacles requires controlled movement rather than raw momentum.

Trophies

There are 24 trophies to collect in Drive Mad. These trophies reward players who keep coming back. They track everything from having 100 total crashes to completing 50 levels in a row without crashing.

There are a number of level-specific trophies as well, such as beating level 1 on your back wheels. These trophies give experienced players a reason to keep pushing long after the main levels stop being enough.

Gold time

Gold time sets a faster target that leaves almost no room for hesitation. One extra correction or a slow landing costs it.

While playing a level, a golden bar at the bottom of the screen counts down to zero. Beat the level before it empties to earn gold time for that stage.

Once a level turns gold, anything less starts to feel unfinished.

About Drive Mad

Drive Mad is an exclusive web game on Poki and has been available to play here since 2022. It has quickly become the most played car game with 18.1 million plays in the US over a 12 month period.

It’s part of the Martin Magni game collection. The game builds on ideas and mechanics first developed on Fancade’s own game engine.

Since its release as a mobile app in 2019, Drive Mad has been played over 300,000,000 times, according to Fancade.

Drive Mad app

In addition to the browser version, Drive Mad is available as a mobile app. You can download it on both iOS and Android.

FAQ

Is Drive Mad hard?
Yes. In later levels, even one bad landing can put the car out of position and make the next obstacle much harder to recover from.
Why do I keep flipping the car?
Most flips happen after overcorrecting, usually right after a run that almost worked. Once the car tips past its balance point, recovery usually isn’t possible, which is why experienced players rely on short inputs instead of panic corrections.
Can you skip levels in Drive Mad?
Yes. After 3 failed attempts, you can watch an ad to skip the level.
Does Drive Mad save your progress?
Yes. Your progress is saved automatically when you play at Poki with an account.
